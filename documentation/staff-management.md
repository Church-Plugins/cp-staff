# Creating Staff Profiles

Each staff member is a staff post: a name, a biography, a photo, contact details, and department assignments.

## Adding a Staff Member

1. Go to **Staff > Add New**.
2. Enter the name in the title field. The placeholder is **Add name**.
3. Add a biography in the content editor.
4. Set a **Featured image**.
5. Fill in **Staff Details**.
6. Click **Publish**.

## Staff Details

**Staff Details** sits under the editor. With the default **Singular Label**, the box title is **Staff Details**.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Title** | Position or role |
| **Email** | Address the contact form sends to |
| **Phone** | Phone number. The staff card links it with `tel:`. |
| **Acronyms** | Credentials or abbreviations |
| **Social** | A network and a profile URL. The networks are Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Twitter (X), Vimeo, and YouTube. Click **Add** for another link, or **Remove** to delete one. |
| **Alternate image** | Image used on the single staff page when the staff member has a featured image |

## Setting Display Order

Staff lists sort by **Order**, then by name. A lower **Order** appears first. Matching **Order** values sort by name.

1. Edit the staff member.
2. Open the actions menu (⋮) next to the staff member's title and choose **Order…**. Set **Order**, then click **Save**.

Use gaps such as 10, 20, and 30 so you can place someone between two people later.

## Assigning Departments

Assign departments in the **Departments** panel:

1. Select one or more departments, or click **Add New Department**.
2. Click **Publish** or **Save**.

A person assigned to more than one department is listed under each of those departments on the archive page.

Department setup is covered in [Departments](departments.md).

## Bulk Management

1. On the staff list, click a department in the **Departments** column.
2. Select the staff members you want to change.
3. Choose **Edit** or **Move to Trash** in **Bulk actions**, then click **Apply**.
4. **Edit** includes a **Departments** checklist.

## Best Practices

- Use photos that are a similar size and quality.
- Keep biographies a similar length.
- Review profiles so names, roles, and contact details stay current.

**Singular Label** and **Plural Label** are set in [Installation](getting-started.md).
